# food-truck-site



## What changes at each breakpoint

- Phones: All menu cards are in 1 column, visit boxes are stacked, and the heading is 2.5rem.

- From md (768px): Cards change to 2 columns, the Visit us boxes are side by side, there's more padding, the heading grows to 4.5rem, and the lead grows to 1.5rem.

- From lg (992px): Cards change to 3 columns, and more space develops on the side in the main area.
